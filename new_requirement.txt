    RFM 
Recency, Frequency, Monetary Value (RFM): Definition

Several other customer segmentation and value models can be used instead of, or alongside, RFM. Each focuses on different dimensions of customer behavior and value.
Key alternatives to RFM
	•	Customer Lifetime Value (CLV/LTV) models: Estimate the total net profit expected from a customer over the entire relationship, often using probabilistic or ML models; this shifts focus from past transactions to expected future value.
	•	Churn / retention models: Predict which customers are likely to stop buying or cancel a subscription using features like activity, complaints, support tickets, and payments; mainly used for proactive retention strategies.
	•	Propensity models: Predict likelihood to perform a specific action (buy, upgrade, respond to a campaign), enabling very granular targeting beyond fixed buckets like “Champions” or “At-risk.”
